Project overview

This repository contains the completed Assignment 1 notebook for MT-2002 Statistical Modeling. The analysis is fully Bayesian and uses PyMC for model construction/sampling with ArviZ for diagnostics and posterior summaries.

Business question

How many requests should the center expect on a typical working day, and how long do selected requests take to resolve?

Data

40 daily request counts and 35 resolution times (minutes), including one unusual 42-minute observation.

Method

Prior specification, MCMC sampling (2 chains), 94% HDIs, posterior predictive checks, and ROPE decisions.

Models fitted

Eight modeling cases move from exploratory diagnostics to final operational recommendations.

Count models

Poisson(λ) with Gamma(3,1) prior, then Zero-Inflated Poisson with Beta(2,2) on ψ to account for excess zero-request days.

Continuous models

Gaussian and Student-t likelihoods for resolution time. Student-t reduces the influence of the 42-minute outlier and yields a tighter scale estimate.
